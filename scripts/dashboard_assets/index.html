READ BEFORE RELYING ON A SIGNAL

Methodology & Risk

PolitiTrack is for paper research and human review. It is not investment advice, does not predict future performance, and never submits brokerage orders. Open the official filing and supporting evidence before acting.

Coverage and delay

Cataloged filings show what the tracker can see. “Cataloged only” records predate transaction backfill and have not necessarily been parsed. Public disclosures may appear weeks after the underlying transaction. Source retention, access restrictions and parser confidence limit coverage. Disclosed amount ranges are not exact dollar exposure.

Scores and Investor Edge

Purchases are bullish research candidates; sales are bearish or caution signals. Scores use deterministic rules, market data, SEC context and structured AI analysis. Only High Priority and Watchlist classifications are presented as actionable for review. Entry bands, chase limits and expiration are review aids, not trade instructions. Prices may be delayed or cached; consult their timestamps.

Investor Edge measures historical benchmark-relative returns from transaction-date and post-disclosure anchors over 5, 20, 60 and 120 trading sessions. Transaction-date returns were not publicly followable. Filer and owner identities remain separate. Sample-size shrinkage, confidence, exclusions and benchmark mappings affect interpretation. Missing outcomes are unavailable, not zero. Insufficient history must not be treated as a recommendation.

Historical alpha is descriptive, not evidence of causation, private information or future results. Followable outcomes do not model notification latency, spread, slippage, fees, taxes or position sizing. Sector mapping can be approximate, and limited observations can dominate results despite weighting and shrinkage.

Paper portfolios and simulations

All positions are simulated. A $10K single-run historical replay is isolated from the production paper portfolio and does not establish a persistent investment record. Independent replay runs cannot be joined into an equity curve. No $20,000 return is promised. Run Simulation is a separate TEST acceptance preview with no live alerts or production deployment.

Operations and local alerts

Run health reflects retained evidence, not an independent live probe. Missing evidence is Unknown. Zero new records is not failure. Browser-local changes, acknowledgements, snoozes, mutes, quiet hours and sound do not modify Gmail, Pushover or Healthchecks. In-page sound is dependable only while this page remains open and active; existing external channels remain the background channels.
