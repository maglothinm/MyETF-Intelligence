Methodology & Risk

Paper research only. Not investment advice; no brokerage orders. Disclosures may be delayed for weeks. Cataloged filings are not necessarily parsed. Open official filings and evidence before relying on a record.

Only High Priority and Watchlist records qualify for the signal board. Scores are evidence-constrained research ranks, not predictions. Investor Edge measures benchmark-relative historical outcomes with separate transaction-date and post-disclosure anchors, filer/owner separation, confidence and sample-size shrinkage. Missing alpha is unavailable, not zero. Low-sample histories are insufficient evidence; inspect the full drilldown.

Prices may be delayed or cached. Entry bands, chase ceilings and expiration are review aids. Historical outcomes omit notification latency, spread, fees, taxes and slippage and do not imply future results. Sector mapping and public-source retention can limit the sample.

All positions are PAPER TRADING. The $10K Agent is a SIMULATED SINGLE-RUN HISTORICAL REPLAY, not persistent portfolio performance. Independent replays cannot form an equity curve; starting capital is not investment gain. There is no promised $20,000 return. The separate Run Simulation is a TEST acceptance preview with no real alerts or production deployment.

Run status uses retained production evidence and freshness windows. Stale means a successful run is too old; missing evidence means Unknown and zero new records is not a failure. Browser-local settings do not change Gmail, Pushover or Healthchecks. In-page sound requires an explicit gesture and is dependable only while this page remains open and active. Existing external channels remain the background channels.
